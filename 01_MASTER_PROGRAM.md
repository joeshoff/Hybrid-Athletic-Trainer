# Hybrid Athletic Training Program — Master Specification

## Athlete

60-year-old male, approximately 5'8", currently approximately 175 lb.

Goal: hybrid athleticism and body recomposition.

Desired characteristics:

- muscular and defined
- strong
- aerobically capable
- athletic
- mobile
- capable of running, biking, hiking/rucking and boxing
- more muscular than a runner
- more aerobic/rangy than a traditional lifter

Long-term body-composition direction: approximately 160 lb while preserving/building muscle.

Do not make the program primarily weight-loss focused.

## Strength

4 core sessions/week.

Generally ~60 minutes.

Typical intensity: RPE 7–8.

Primary lifts: 3–5 × 4–10
 Secondary: 3–4 × 6–12
 Accessories: 2–4 × 8–15+

Use progressive overload.

Progress primarily through:

1. reps
2. load
3. sets when appropriate
4. difficulty
5. execution/quality

Avoid routine failure training.

## Constraints

Bulgarian split squats are not standard programming.

Knees require intelligent loading.

Right ankle limits some squat patterns.

Barbell squats are occasional rather than primary.

Avoid excessive high-impact leg work.

Avoid excessive steep treadmill incline.

Avoid destroying legs at the expense of cardio.

60-lb vest work is high-load conditioning, not ordinary Zone 2.

Do not routinely run with the 60-lb vest.

Do not increase vest load and duration simultaneously.

## Programming Principle

Stable movement families should remain long enough to progress.

Typical block length: 3–6 weeks.

Rotate exercises strategically for:

- stimulus
- joint management
- stagnation
- equipment utilization

Do not rotate simply for novelty.

## Recovery Priority

When conflicts occur:

1. Core strength
2. Weekly VO2 stimulus
3. Optional/high-impact conditioning
4. Saturday wild card

Do not routinely combine maximal lower-body strength with maximal running.

## Coaching Standard

The coach owns:

- programming

- cardio prescription
- recovery adjustments
- exercise substitutions
- interpretation of performance/fatigue

SmartGym owns:
